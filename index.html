Our Story

From Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh to INDIA - Clean Fuel, Indian-made

The journey of childhood friends Ramu Aradhyula & Ajindar Singh Bagga.

What began as a shared dream between two childhood friends has grown into one of Central India's most trusted names in sustainable fuel. In 2011, Ramu Aradhyula and Ajindar Singh Bagga set up their first biomass briquette manufacturing facility in Raipur, Chhattisgarh — not just to build a business, but to answer a question that had been nagging them for years: why is India burning foreign fuel when its own fields produce enough to power itself?

Over a decade and a half of refining processes, building trust with industry leaders, and watching agricultural waste transform into reliable industrial warmth, the answer is now clear. And so is their next step.

Today, Ramu and Ajindar with Anmol Aradhyula and B Sridhar are expanding into Bobbili, Andhra Pradesh with a state-of-the-art Biomass Pellet manufacturing plant — bringing the same commitment to quality, sustainability, and Indian self-reliance to the southern industrial corridor.

Our Vision

To reduce industrial pollution and convert agricultural waste into clean, reliable energy — building a greener India, one pellet at a time.

Our Mission

To make Indian industries — especially the hospitality and food sectors — fully self-sufficient in fuel by replacing costly imported gas with high-performance, locally-produced biomass pellets, reducing crores in foreign exchange outflow and strengthening India's energy backbone.

Atmanirbhar Bharat

Atmanirbhar Bharat — in every pellet

India spends crores of rupees every year importing gas fuels that its own land can replace. We believe hotels, food processing units, and industrial kitchens should not be dependent on foreign supply chains. Our biomass pellets are made from local agricultural residue, priced competitively, and available reliably — because true self-reliance starts at the factory floor.
